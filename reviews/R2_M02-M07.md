# Referee report rv2: blueprint modules M02–M07

Ortega-Cerdà, *Multipliers and weighted ∂̄-estimates*, RMI 18 (2002) 355–377.
Docs refereed: `blueprint/M02_Potentials.md`, `M03_Doubling.md`, `M04_Regularization.md`, `M05_Partition.md`, `M06_Quadrature.md`, `M07_LogModulus.md`. I also read the README, `Defs.lean`, `Quadrature.lean` and `LogModulus.lean`, the interface sections of M08 (§1, §2.1, §3.2–3.11, §4) and the "assumed" sections of M11, M12 and M13.

Every paper claim below was read from 160-dpi renders of journal pp. 357–375 (PDF pp. 3–21).

Numerical checks are in `scratchpad/rv2_scripts/rv2_kernels.py`:
- the identity `kDisc_eq` holds to a relative error of 2·10⁻¹⁴;
- the sharp Andersson–Pascuas ratio is 0.117, against the doc's constant 4/π;
- with the printed sign, the plane kernel decays like `R⁻²`; with the corrected sign, like `R⁻³`.

**Overall verdict.** The mathematics of M02–M07 is sound. I found no CRITICAL problem: no false statement and no wrong inequality in a proof that I could not repair locally. I re-derived every displayed constant that carries weight (listed in the last section), and they are correct.

There are **two MAJOR interface gaps**:
- the uniform (`_quant`) exports of M03 and M05 that M08 and M09 consume do not exist yet;
- M02's Weyl lemma is weaker than what M07, which is already proved in Lean, and M13 consume.

Most claimed errata are confirmed. One is refuted: the claim that Lemma 2's projected measure is not doubling.

## A. MAJOR findings

### 1. MAJOR: M03 and M05 do not export the uniform (`_quant`) statements that M08/M09 consume
- **Location.** README §3a.6; M03 §2 (all statements); M05 §2.3 (`lemma4_*`); M08 §4.1 and risk R4; M09 header. The paper itself has no uniform statements; this is a formalization design point.
- **Problem.**
  - Every constant-producing M03 lemma (`claim1`, `decay`, `christ_lemma23`, `measure_ball_le_rpow`, `rpow_le_measure_ball`, `massRadiusM_inv_smul`, `measure_*_massRadius*_bounds`, `lemma3`, …) has the form `(hd : IsLocDoublingM μ) … : ∃ C, …`. The constant is chosen **after** `μ`, and the abstract layer is parametrised by `IsLocDoublingM μ := ∃ C, …`.
  - `lemma4_plane/disc` (M05) likewise have `∃ a A L` after `μ`, and so does the internal `exists_rect_partition` (`∃ L`).
  - M08 §4.1 requires `MeasClass*.lapNorm`, `massRadius*_lapNorm_quant`, `claim1_*_quant`, `measure_*_massRadius_quant`, `decay_*_quant`, `christ_*_quant` and `lemma4_*_quant`, all of the form `∀ Cd R₀, ∃ C, ∀ ν in class, …`.
  - These do not follow formally from the per-measure versions (`∀μ∃C ⇏ ∃C∀μ`). The whole `_quant` chain is blocked, including Theorem 4, Proposition 2 and `theorem2_uniform`, and with it the BOC bridge (M11 §8.3).
- **Mathematics: fine.** I checked that every constant in M03 §3 and M05 §3 depends only on:
  - the local doubling constant `C`;
  - the scale `R₁` and the mass radius `R₀` (and `N`);
  - the covering numbers of the geometry.

  The relevant steps are the scale-up §3.2, Lemma 1 §3.4–3.7, Claim 1 §3.12, decay §3.13, Lemma 3 §3.14, Chr 2.3 §3.15, growth §3.16 and `massRadiusM_inv_smul`. In M05 they are `C₀`, `C_E`, `κ`, `k₀`, `L`, `Λ_c(R')`, `a`, `A`. Positivity of `ρ` is not uniform, but M08 only needs the qualitative `0 < ρ`.
- **Fix.** This is a restatement, but it must be done before scaffolding.
  1. In `Doubling/Abstract.lean` define `IsLocDoublingWithM μ (C : ℝ≥0)` and `IsLocDoublingM μ := ∃ C, IsLocDoublingWithM μ C`.
  2. State each constant-producing lemma as `∀ (C : ℝ≥0) (R₁ R₀ …), ∃ consts, ∀ μ, IsLocDoublingWithM μ C → hfin → (∀ x, 1 ≤ μ (ball x R₀)) → …`.
  3. Keep the current forms as one-line corollaries.
  4. Add an "Export (quantitative)" subsection to M03 §2.4 with exactly the M08 §4.1 signatures, using M08's classes `MeasClassPlane/Disc`, `IsLocallyDoublingWith`, `lapNorm`. Add `lemma4_plane_quant` and `lemma4_disc_quant` to M05 §2.3.
  5. Make `exists_rect_partition` output an explicit `L(C₀, C_E, κ)`, for example a named `def rectL`.
  6. Note that M08 uses `1 < ν(B(z,R₀))` while M03 uses `1 ≤`. The strict form implies the weak one, so this is harmless.

### 2. MAJOR: M02's Weyl lemma is too weak for M07 (proved in Lean) and M13
- **Location.**
  - M02 §2 (statement and the closing "Remark (`L¹_loc` version, not needed)") and §5 (exports to M07);
  - M07 §3b and §4, and `LogModulus.lean` `IsWeakLogModulus.ballwise`;
  - M13 §4.2. The paper's corresponding step is p. 366.
- **Problem.**
  - M02 proves `harmonicOnNhd_of_weakHarmonic` only for `ContinuousOn φ Ω`.
  - `LogModulus.lean` consumes `WeylLaplacianStmt`: `LocallyIntegrableOn f U`, weakly harmonic ⇒ `∃ u` harmonic with `f = u` a.e. It applies this to `f = v − Σ_{ζ∈F} n ζ log|·−ζ|` on `ball c r`, and this `f` is only known to be continuous on `ball c r \ F`. The node singularities cancel only in M08's concrete `v`, not in the abstract API.
  - M02's justification ("the functions M07 applies Weyl to are continuous off the singular set") does not meet its own hypothesis `ContinuousOn φ Ω`.
  - M13 §4.2 also assumes `weylLaplacian : WeylLaplacianStmt`.
  - M07 §7's "discharge the two hypotheses (≈5 lines)" therefore fails for `WeylLaplacianStmt`. It does succeed for `LogKernelStmt`, using `locallyIntegrable_log_norm_sub` and `integral_log_norm_sub_mul_laplacian`, since `C^∞ ⊆ C²`.
- **Fix.** Promote M02's remark to a theorem `weylLaplacian : WeylLaplacianStmt`. It suffices to do this for `U = ball c r`, which is all M07 uses, and doing so avoids a gluing step over general open sets.
  1. Fix `z₀` and `R` with `B(z₀,3R) ⊆ U`, `s < R`, and `β ∈ C_c^∞(B(z₀,R))`.
  2. Since `ΔG_s = k_s − δ₀`, we have `Δ(G_s⋆β) = k_s⋆β − β`, and `G_s⋆β ∈ C_c^∞(B(z₀,2R))`. Use `HasCompactSupport.contDiff_convolution_left`, with `G_s` integrable and compactly supported.
  3. So `∫ f β = ∫ f (k_s⋆β) = ∫ (k_s⋆f̃) β` by Fubini and the radial symmetry of `k_s`, where `f̃ = 1_{B(z₀,3R)} f`.
  4. Hence `f = k_s⋆f̃` a.e. on `B(z₀,R)` (`IsOpen.ae_eq_zero_of_integral_contDiff_smul_eq_zero`).
  5. `k_s⋆f̃` is `C^∞`, and §2 Steps 4–5 show it is harmonic.
  6. On a ball, the local representatives agree on overlaps (continuous and a.e. equal), so they glue to one harmonic `u`.

  Only the ball case `U = ball c r` is needed. The gluing is then just "continuous local representatives that agree a.e. agree on overlaps", so this is a few dozen lines on top of M02 §2 and §4.2, not a general-open-set theorem.

  Also add to M02 §1 the two kernel lemmas that M13 §4.2 assumes and M08 §3.8(7) uses implicitly:
  - `integrableOn_log_norm_sub_uniform` (a uniform `L¹(K)` bound of `log|·−a|` for `a ∈ K'` compact);
  - `integral_logPotential_mul_laplacian` (Fubini plus the fundamental solution).

  Both are short. The alternative of adding to `IsWeakLogModulus` a field saying that `v − Σ n log|·−ζ|` is continuous across the nodes is possible, but it pushes junk-value bookkeeping onto M08. I do not recommend it.

## B. The claimed errata of the paper

### 3. ERRATUM-CONFIRMED: plane kernel sign (p. 371, display `k(z,ζ) = (1/4π) log|z−ζ|² − (1/2π)(1−χ_{D(0,1)}(ζ)) Re(ln|ζ| − z/ζ + ½ z²/ζ²)`)
- **Location.** M02 §0, §6.1 and Appendix A.4.
- **Evidence.**
  - `log|ζ−z| = log|ζ| − Re(z/ζ) − ½Re(z²/ζ²) − …`, so the subtracted Taylor polynomial needs `−½z²/ζ²`.
  - With the printed `+`, `k = −(1/2π)Re(z²/ζ²) + O(|z/ζ|³)`, which is not absolutely integrable against `ν(D(0,r)) ≍ r²`.
  - Numerically, `max_θ|k(z, Re^{iθ})|` is `9.5·10⁻⁴, 9.3·10⁻⁶, 9.2·10⁻⁸` with the printed sign, against `2.5·10⁻⁵, 2.4·10⁻⁸, 2.3·10⁻¹¹` corrected, for `R = 10, 100, 1000`.
  - `kPlane` in Appendix A and `abs_kPlane_le` (`(3π)⁻¹|z/ζ|³`) are correct.
- **Fix.** None needed; the record is right.

### 4. ERRATUM-CONFIRMED, with a refinement in 𝔻: the commutation step (p. 371, last display `|K[μ₁] − K[μ̃₁]| = |K[μ₁] − K[μ₁] ⋆ χ_{D(0,2R)}/|D(0,2R)||`)
- **Location.** M02 §0, §6.2 and §6.3; M04 §9.4.
- **Plane: confirmed.**
  - `K[μ₁] − K[μ̃₁] = ∫ (k(z,ζ) − avg_{|t|<2R} k(z,ζ+t)) dμ₁(ζ)`.
  - The logarithmic part gives exactly the paper's `∫_{D(z,2R)} log(2R/d) dμ₁`.
  - The correction `c(z,·)` is harmonic in `ζ` on `D(ζ,2R)` when `|ζ| > 1+2R`. The remaining `|ζ| ≤ 1+2R` contributes a harmonic polynomial `a + Re(bz + cz²)`, generically nonzero, so `sup|φ−ψ| = ∞` as written.
  - This is a gap in the *proof* of Lemma 6, not a counterexample to Lemma 6: subtracting the polynomial does not change `Δψ`. M02 already says this, and M04 §9.4 could repeat it.
- **Disc: confirmed, but the stated reason should be sharpened.** Write `k = (1/4π)[log ρ² + (1−ρ²) + T]` with `T = |z|²(1−|ζ|²)²/|1−ζ̄z|²` (`kDisc_eq`, verified).
  - The two Möbius-invariant parts `log ρ²` and `1−ρ²` **do** commute with the invariant convolution, since convolutions of radial functions on the disc commute (Gelfand pair). Only the non-invariant term `T` breaks the commutation.
  - `1−ρ²` is not harmonic, so the paper's mean-value reduction does not apply to it either. But `Δ̃_ζ(1−ρ²) = −4(1−ρ²)²`, so its averaging error is `O((1−ρ²)²)`, which is summable against `μ₁`.
  - For `T`, the averaging error is of the order of `Δ̃_ζ c`, where `c = (1−ρ²) + T` is the whole correction (see the first bullet of this list). Since `∫_𝔻 |Δ_ζ c(z,ζ)| dA(ζ) ≍ log 1/(1−|z|)`, the bound is plausibly false, not merely unproved, for non-uniform `μ₁`.
  - M02 §6.3 ("neither invariant nor harmonic") is correct but imprecise.
- **Fix.** Reword M02 §6.3: "the invariant parts commute; the non-invariant term `|z|²(1−|ζ|²)²/|1−ζ̄z|²` does not, and its averaging error is not controlled by [Chr91, 2.3]". This is optional; the formal route (local potentials) is unaffected.

### 5. ERRATUM-CONFIRMED: growth class in the disc (p. 371, "`μ₁(D(0,r)) ≲ (1−r)⁻²` in the disk")
- **Location.** M02 §6.3 and A.5; M04 §9.5.
- **Evidence.**
  - The statement is true but too weak. By layer cake, `∫(1−|ζ|)² dν = ∫₀¹ 2(1−r) ν(D(0,r)) dr`, which diverges under `(1−r)⁻²` growth. Example: `ν = (1−|ζ|)⁻³ dm`.
  - Since `|kDisc(0,ζ)| ≍ (1−|ζ|²)²`, `K[ν]` really diverges for positive `ν` of that growth.
  - The true growth of the paper's `μ₁` (mass 1 per hyperbolic tile) is `≍ (1−r)⁻¹`, which suffices.
- **Fix.** None needed. The Andersson–Pascuas bound with constant `4/π` (A.3) is correct; I re-derived both cases.

### 6. ERRATUM-CONFIRMED: Lemma 1's attributions are swapped (p. 360, proof of Lemma 1)
- **Location.** M03 §1 and §5.1.
- **Evidence.**
  - Printed: "The left inequality is essentially [Chr91, Lemma 2.1] and the right inequality follows directly from the definition."
  - The left inequality `(μB/μB')^γ ≲ r(B)/r(B')` is an upper bound for the mass ratio. It follows from iterated doubling, `γ = 1/log₂C`.
  - The right inequality `r(B)/r(B') ≲ (μB/μB')^{1/γ}` is reverse doubling. It needs the annulus to contain a ball (connectedness and unboundedness), which is the content of [Chr91, 2.1]. It does **not** follow from the definition.
  - Lemma 3's proof (p. 362) uses the left inequality to bound `ρ(z)/(2ⁿρ(w))`, and reverse doubling for the final sum.
  - I could not consult [Chr91] (not in `literature/`). The erratum stands regardless of exactly what [Chr91, 2.1] states.
- **Fix.** None needed. Also note p. 360's restriction `r(B) < 1` needs the scale-up (M03 §0.2, §5.2), which is correct as written.

### 7. ERRATUM-REFUTED (overstated): "Lemma 2's projected measure ν is not literally doubling" (p. 360, proof of Lemma 2, Figure 1)
- **Location.** M03 §0.4 and §5.3.
- **Why refuted.** Let the square be `S = J × [0,L]` and `ν(A) = μ(A × [0,L])`, with `r ≤ min(L/2, 1/6)`.
  - Cover the wide strip `I(x,2r) × [0,L]` by the balls `B(c_j, 3r)`. The centres `c_j = (x, jr)`, `1 ≤ j ≤ L/r − 1`, sit on the centre line at distance `≥ r` from the top and bottom edges.
  - Then `B(c_j, 3r/4) ⊆ I(x,r) × [0,L]`, these balls overlap at most twice, and local doubling gives `ν(I(x,2r)) ≤ 2C² ν(I(x,r))`.
  - Intervals that stick out of `J` are handled the same way, using the half of `I(x,r)` inside `J`.
  - So `ν` is locally doubling on `J`. "Doubling ⇒ no atoms" then gives `μ(I') = ν({x₀}) = 0`.
  - **Caveat.** The covering balls `B(c_j, 3r)` must lie in `Ω` for the paper's doubling definition to apply. This is automatic in the formalized domains (`Ω = ℂ`, and hyperbolic balls in `𝔻`). For a general `Ω`, shrink `I'` so that the square has a margin inside `Ω`.
  - The paper's argument is correct up to this routine verification.
- **Fix.** Reword M03 §0.4 and §5.3 as "the paper omits the (routine) verification that ν is doubling at scales `≲ |I'|`", not as an error. The porosity route (§3.9) remains a good design choice: one lemma covers segments, circles, arcs, horocycles, geodesic arcs and hyperbolic spheres. I checked §3.9; the 4s-separated maximal set, the shells at `s_n = s₀(κ/4)ⁿ` and the doubling count `k = ⌈log₂(10/κ)⌉` are all correct.

### 11. ERRATUM-CONFIRMED, and strengthened: Lemma 5 "inside R" is false (pp. 365–366)
- **Location.** M06 §1.
- **The counterexample is valid.** Take `R = [−1,1]²`, `m = 3` and `μ = N(wδ₋₁ + (1−w)δ₁)` with `w` irrational. Then `Σλᵢ² = N` with `Re λ² ≤ 1` on `R` forces `λᵢ = ±1`, so `Σλᵢ ∈ ℤ`, while `∫z dμ = N(1−2w) ∉ ℤ`.
- **Strengthening.** Because the printed `k` depends only on `(m, C)`, a compactness argument shows the statement fails **also for absolutely continuous measures**.
  - Let `μ_n` be uniform densities on `R ∩ D(±1, 1/n)` with the same weights.
  - The node sets lie in the compact `R^N`. Along a subsequence they converge, and their moments converge to those of `μ`, which is impossible.
  - So the failure is not an artifact of atoms. Whether a version with `k` depending on the doubling constant holds remains open, as M06 says.
- **Lean.** The statements of `lemma5` and `lemma5_moments` in `Quadrature.lean` match M06 §2 verbatim. They have no junk-value issues: `IsFiniteMeasure` is derived from `hmass`, and the bounded support makes every Bochner moment genuine.
- **Fix.** Optionally add the a.c. remark to M06 §1 and the NOTEBOOK.

## C. Design decisions (README §3a)

### 12. CONFIRMED CORRECT: moment order `m = 3` in 2D and `m = 2` on ℝ via area counting
- **Location.** README §3a.1; M08 §3.4–3.6; M03 §3.13; M13 §0.1.
- **The near-field argument (M08 §3.5) is correct.**
  1. **Ring counts.** In each dyadic ring around `z`, the disjoint inner balls give `Σ_{F_n}(ρ_i/d)² ≤ 16 sinh²(1)/η²`, by Lebesgue measure or hyperbolic area.
  2. **Decay.** The decay lemma `ρ(ζ)/d ≲ (ρ(z)/d)^α` gives `max_{F_n} ρ_i/d ≲ 2^{−nα}`.
  3. **Conclusion.** So `Σ(ρ_i/d)³ ≲ Σ_n 2^{−nα} < ∞` for any `α > 0`, i.e. for every locally doubling measure.
- **Why `m = 2` fails in 2D.** Each ring then contributes `O(1)`, over `≈ log(1/ρ)` rings.
- **Far field.** The cell-area Fubini argument (plane) and Forelli–Rudin (disc, order 2) need nothing more.
- **On ℝ.** Matching the real moments 0 and 1 kills the linear Taylor term, because `Re((t−c)/(z−c)) = (t−c)·Re(1/(z−c))` is linear in real `t`. There are linearly many cells per ring, and sublinear lengths.
- **What is lost.** Nothing in Theorem 4. Only the paper's optional Remark (`M = 1`, separated zeros) is lost, and it is not used.
- **Two wording fixes (MINOR).**
  - README §3a.1 attributes the decay to "the decay estimate inside the proof of Lemma 3". It is M03 §3.13 `decay`, which uses both halves of Lemma 1 plus Lipschitz `ρ`.
  - M03 §4.1 lists `lemma3_*` as consumed by M08, while M08 §5.5 says Lemma 3 is unused. Make them consistent; keep `lemma3_paper` only as a fidelity theorem.

### 13. CONFIRMED CORRECT: horocyclic boxes in the disc

- **Location.** M04 §2.1 and §2.3; M11 §2.1; M12 §2.1.
